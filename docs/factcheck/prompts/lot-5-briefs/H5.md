## Coordination signals (`core/factcheck/coordination.py`)
`async def coordination_signals(session, *, group_id: int, now) -> list[dict]` — each
`{kind, count, window_minutes, detail}`, informative only:
- `near_identical_burst`: ≥ 10 distinct author accounts posted copies (same `copy_of_id`
  root or same `content_hash`) of the group's contents within 10 minutes;
- `new_accounts`: share of authors whose `platform_accounts.created_at` (first seen by us)
  is within 7 days ≥ 50 % with ≥ 10 authors (say in `detail` it is "first seen", not the
  real account age);
- `installation_burst`: one installation pseudonym produced ≥ 20 observations of the
  group's contents within 10 minutes;
- `media_reuse`: one media asset attached to contents of ≥ 2 different groups (captions
  may contradict: the team checks).
Never any political side, never a verdict input. Bounded queries.

## Gate (English tags; ids as strings)
- `GET /v1/narratives?state=&limit=` (`dossiers:read`): id, title (+status), state,
  theme, groups count, last 1 h stats (share, accounts, platforms), opened/last activity.
- `GET /v1/narratives/{id}` (`dossiers:read`): + medoids, groups (id, level, verdict,
  light signal, reach), stats of the 3 windows (last 12 points each), journal (50),
  representative group, previous narrative, split suggested, novelty fingerprints that
  took (top 10).
- `POST /v1/narratives/{target}/merge` `{source_id, reason}`, `POST
  /v1/narratives/{id}/split` `{group_ids, reason}` (`claim_groups:manage`),
  `POST /v1/narratives/{id}/title` `{title?}` (`dossiers:review`: validates the draft
  title or sets a corrected one).
- Draft view: `narrative` {id, title, state, groups} when the group has one, and
  `coordination` (signals above). `GET /v1/groups/{id}`: add `narrative` id/state.

## Bot
- Notification on `narrative.surge_detected` (tier `surge` and `alert`; the alert
  mentions the team role if the notifier supports it) and `narrative.surge_ended`
  (report), in the dossiers channel, both locales, idempotent like existing notifications.
- Commands `/narrative list [state]`, `/narrative show id`, `/narrative merge target
  source reason`, `/narrative split id groups reason`, `/narrative title id [title]`.
- Draft message: narrative line (title, state) and coordination block ("informational,
  never a verdict input").
- i18n fr/en with the parity test.
