## Attachment (`on_group_created` → job `attach_narrative`)
Subscription: enqueue `factcheck.attach_narrative` (`dedupe_key=attach_key(group)`), no
query beyond what the payload gives (group public id → resolve in the job). Job:
candidates = narratives (active/watch/surge/alert, not merged) of the 10 nearest groups
(`nearest_groups` with the group's representative vector); compute the 4 signals and the
anchors (CONTRACT5 decision 8) with SQL (`content_claims`, `content_items.author_account_id`,
`claims.entities`, `content_media`, links found by regex in the contents' text); evaluate
with the pure rule; attach to the best passing narrative (most signals, then highest
medoid similarity) or found a new narrative (`state='active'`, founder = medoids =
[group]); a match only on a closed narrative ⇒ new narrative with
`previous_narrative_id`. Update `entity_rarity` (+1 per entity of the group's claims for
today) and `last_activity_at`. Journal (`narrative_events`), `narrative_groups.signals`.
Grouping never uses the author's identity (accounts are only counted for co-occurrence).

## Maintenance (`maintain_narratives`, hourly)
Close narratives inactive for `fc_narrative_close_days` (state `closed`, event); re-elect
medoids at most once a day (founder fixed + 2 highest-reach groups); flag
`split_suggested` above `fc_narrative_split_groups` groups or when a single group bridges
two otherwise disconnected subsets (use co-occurrence/semantic links; keep it simple and
bounded: only for narratives ≤ 200 groups); purge `narrative_stats` older than 30 days and
`entity_rarity` older than 31 days.

## Waves (`waves_step(session, settings, now) -> dict`, called by the scheduler pass)
For narratives with activity in the last 6 h: window stats (5 min, 1 h, 6 h): posts =
observations in the window of contents linked to the narrative's active groups (via
`content_claims` and `claim_group_members`), flow = all observations in the window,
share, distinct author accounts, platforms, reach; Poisson score versus the previous
equal window scaled by the flow ratio; insert `narrative_stats`. Tier (pure function,
CONTRACT5 decision 9; growth ignored for 24 h after the newest `extension_filter_versions`
row). Transitions via the use case with events: entering surge/alert ⇒
`narrative.surge_detected`; set `surge_started_at`; raise the groups (N2, alert N4 with
`promote_to_n4` on the representative), choose the representative (highest reach, then
claim with a value) and `request_investigation` on it if it has no running/finished one
(jobs high priority: pass `priority=10` to `enqueue` if the investigation enqueue supports
it; otherwise note it); enqueue light checks (`factcheck.light_check`, payload with
`dossier_investigation_id` of the representative investigation once it has kept passages)
for the other groups; enqueue `factcheck.narrative_title` when the narrative reaches watch
without a title. Leaving: below watch for `fc_surge_end_hours` ⇒ state `active`,
`narrative.surge_ended` with the report (duration, posts, platforms, cost of the
narrative's investigations, delay from `surge_started_at` to the first draft of its groups).
Return a dict of counts. Bounded: at most 500 narratives per pass, all SQL aggregates.

## Surge quota and TTL
- `assemble`: a draft of a group whose narrative is at surge/alert counts against
  `fc_surge_daily_quota` (separate counter: verdict `flags.surge = true`), not the daily
  quota; when the surge quota is full it falls back to the daily quota rules.
- `_expiry_of`: a verdict validated while its group's narrative is at surge/alert expires
  at most `fc_surge_verdict_ttl_hours` after validation.

## Sampling and novelty (`filter_for_surges`)
For the given contents, those whose E1 vector has cosine ≥ 0.85 with a medoid of a
narrative at surge/alert (load medoid vectors once per call; at most 20 surging
narratives) are subject to sampling: per narrative, if the last minute's rate (from
`content_extractions` + candidates) exceeds `fc_sampling_per_minute`, keep a stratified
random sample (platform, hour) at rate `limit ÷ rate`; the others are returned in the map
(sampled out). For every sampled-out content compute novelty fingerprints (pure function)
and upsert `narrative_novelty` (posts, accounts up to 10 ids, best content by
engagement); a fingerprint that takes (CONTRACT5 decision 10) forces its best content into
the extraction list (`forced_at`), within `fc_novelty_budget_share` of the narrative's
extractions of the last hour. RNG injectable for tests.
